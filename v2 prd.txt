# 1. Product Overview

Swavik Fabric Perfumes is a premium luxury fragrance and fabric perfume brand originating from Kannauj, India.

The platform functions as both:

* A luxury digital showroom
* A direct-to-consumer e-commerce store

The experience should communicate:

* Heritage
* Craftsmanship
* Exclusivity
* Modern luxury

The visual and experiential quality should be comparable to premium fragrance brands such as:

* Isak Fragrances
* Ajmal Perfumes
* Adil Qadri
* Raahi Parfums
